A permanent, readable library.

This site is designed to be fast, stable, and long-lived: the public face is simple, and the deep archive lives as static HTML.

How to navigate

The Home page is a mosaic: each tile represents a field of Ted’s work and jumps to the corresponding section on the page. The archive is accessible via the archive link in the top navigation.

Mosaic home Open archive

Why static

Static hosting avoids plugin risk and database complexity. The archive can preserve everything without the fragility of a live CMS.

Contact
